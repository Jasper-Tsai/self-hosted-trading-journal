# Code of Conduct

This project follows a practical maintainer standard:

- Be respectful and specific
- Assume contributors are working in good faith
- Keep discussions focused on the project
- Do not share personal, account, broker, or trading data in public threads
- Do not pressure maintainers or contributors for financial, trading, or personal advice

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, spam, and posting private data without consent.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove issues, pull requests, discussions, or comments that violate these expectations.
